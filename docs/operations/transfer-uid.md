# Finding a UID for resident transfer

The recipient's UID is shown in the iOS app:

1. Recipient opens `設定` → `帳號`.
2. Finds `我的 UID`.
3. Taps the copy icon.
4. Sends the UID to the transfer initiator.

The UID is the 苔間 profile UUID. It is not the LINE user ID, email address, or
display name. The transfer screen also has a `如何取得對方 UID？` help dialog.

The transfer initiator opens the resident → `轉移居民` → `其他使用者`, pastes the
UID, adds an optional note, and sends the invitation. The recipient accepts it
under `設定` → `家庭` → `轉移邀請`. Invitations expire after 7 days.
